## Unreleased

## 0.1.0

First public version.

1. Live mode: `annotate` and `annotateGroup` draw marks on any page.
2. Step player, static pictures (`drawScene`) and paused scenes (`mountScene`).
3. Marks: arrow, box, bracket, circle, highlight, label, scribble, strike and underline.
4. Ten built-in styles, grain textures and a font list.
5. CLI: `targets`, `grid`, `validate`, `preview`, `play` and `render` (MP4).
